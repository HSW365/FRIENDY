Good questions.

Am I talking to a real person?

No. Maya, Dre, Sage, Kai and Nova are AI companions. They are built to listen, encourage and help you see things differently, and they will always tell you they are AI if you ask.

Is this therapy?

No. Friendy is a supportive companion, not a therapist, doctor or crisis service, and it does not diagnose or treat anything. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

How does paying with Cash App work?

Pick a plan and you'll get an order code. Send the plan amount to $hsw365 on Cash App with that code in the note. We match the payment to your account and unlock your friends. Each payment covers 30 days and nothing renews on its own.

Are my conversations private?

Your chats are saved to your account so your friends can keep up with your story, and only you can see them when signed in. Messages are processed by our AI provider to write replies. You can clear a conversation or delete your whole account at any time from your account screen.

Can I switch plans or stop?

Yes. Cash App plans simply end after 30 days unless you renew. Card subscriptions renew monthly until you cancel; email us and we'll take care of it.

Who can use Friendy?

Friendy is for adults 18 and older.
